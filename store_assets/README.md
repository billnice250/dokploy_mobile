Generated from the DployGo 1.0.0 demo data. The screenshots contain no real
server addresses, credentials, customer data, or debug banners. All PNGs are
flattened to opaque sRGB artwork for store-upload compatibility.

## App Store Connect

- `app-store/icon/app-icon-1024.png` — 1024 × 1024 App Store icon.
- `app-store/screenshots/iphone-6.9/` — 1320 × 2868 portrait screenshots.
- `app-store/screenshots/ipad-13/` — 2064 × 2752 portrait screenshots.

Apple accepts one to ten screenshots. Upload the five files in filename order.
The highest-resolution iPhone and iPad sets can be scaled by App Store Connect
for smaller displays.

## Google Play Console

- `play-store/icon/icon-512.png` — 512 × 512, full-square PNG.
- `play-store/feature-graphic/feature-graphic-1024x500.png` — 1024 × 500,
  opaque PNG.
- `play-store/screenshots/phone/` — portrait phone screenshots.
- `play-store/screenshots/tablet-7/` — 7-inch tablet screenshots.
- `play-store/screenshots/tablet-10/` — 10-inch tablet screenshots.

Upload the files in filename order. DployGo does not target Android TV, Wear
OS, Automotive, Chromebook-only, or XR form factors, so assets for those
device classes are intentionally not included.

## Regeneration

Store artwork is generated by `scripts/generate_store_artwork.swift`. Product
screenshots are raw captures from Flutter running with its built-in demo
repository, then copied into this folder at the store-specific dimensions. The
artwork generator refreshes the shared DployGo mark in every stored screenshot
so launcher, in-app, store, and documentation branding stay synchronized.
